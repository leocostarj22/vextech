Landscape 1536x1024.

LAYOUT: a 2x2 contact sheet of FOUR separate photographs that fill the canvas exactly, each photograph occupying one quadrant (768x512 each), edge to edge, with NO gutters, NO borders, NO white frame, NO captions. Hard straight edges between the quadrants exactly at the horizontal and vertical center lines. Each quadrant is a different object and a different setting, but all four share the same art direction.

TOP-LEFT: a large outdoor banner on a building wall with a giant deep green leaf mark, palm trees in front
TOP-RIGHT: an open book spread with a full-page photograph of the sea and a small caption block
BOTTOM-LEFT: a white ceramic mug with a tiny terracotta sun mark, steam, on concrete
BOTTOM-RIGHT: a paper shopping bag in kraft with a black arch mark, standing on the floor against a plaster wall

STYLE (shared by the whole series): editorial photograph of finished brand design work by a small graphic design studio in Recife, Brazil. Quiet, gallery-like, art-directed. Settings: warm off-white walls (#f2f0eb), raw concrete, light plaster, pale wood, a few tropical leaves casting soft shadows. Natural soft daylight, gentle shadows, shallow depth of field, slight film grain. Restrained palette: warm off-white #f2f0eb, terracotta #d9653b, deep forest green #2f4a3a, near-black #141414, kraft paper brown, sand. Graphic language of the brands: bold flat geometric marks built from arches, half circles, quarter circles and leaf shapes, large and simple. Photographed straight-on or at a gentle angle, object centered with breathing room. Must look like a real portfolio photo from a top design studio. No real brands, no real logos. Any visible lettering must be only the fictional name given for that object, spelled exactly; otherwise use tiny illegible marks. No people's faces.
